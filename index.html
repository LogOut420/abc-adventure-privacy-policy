ABC Adventure - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

ABC Adventure is a children's educational game designed to help children learn the English alphabet through interactive activities and games.

Information We Collect

ABC Adventure does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or precise location information.

Children's Privacy

ABC Adventure is designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Data Stored on the Device

Game progress, stars, settings, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on the user's device so that progress can be maintained between app sessions.

This locally stored gameplay information is not intentionally transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The current version of ABC Adventure does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect personal information.

Internet Access

The core learning and gameplay features are designed to work without requiring a user account or continuous internet access.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or ABC Adventure, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
